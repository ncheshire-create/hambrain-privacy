# Privacy Policy for HamBrain

Last updated: October 2026

Zbyt Studios ("we", "us", or "our") operates the HamBrain mobile application (the "App"), a study aid for the FCC Amateur Radio Technician (Element 2) exam.

## Information We Collect

HamBrain does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The App works entirely offline and does not require an internet connection. No personal data is collected. No usage analytics are tracked. No advertising identifiers are used. No data is shared with third parties.

All app data (practice progress, mock exam scores, streaks) is stored locally on your device only. Deleting the App removes this data.

## Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect any information from children or any other users.

## Affiliation

HamBrain is an independent study aid and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the FCC, ARRL, or NCVEC.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
